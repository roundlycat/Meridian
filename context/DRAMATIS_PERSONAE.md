## Objects with Roles

**The Oxygen Mini** — MIDI controller, centred on the desk. Planned as additional input surface for the ecology.

**The Xbox Elite Controller** — Haptic grammar designed: left motor = system uncertainty, right motor = model confidence, impulse triggers as confidence metaphor, underside paddles as mode-layer switches. Not yet fully implemented.

**The Foot Pedals** — Attached, speaking to the system. Proposed as base interaction grammar: single impulse = acknowledge/proceed, double tap = pause/hold, sustained = no/back up, left/right asymmetry mirroring haptic grammar. Root gestures that learn meaning through use.

**The Meta Quest 3** — Arrived today. Fresh out of box. First setup in progress. Will become the AR viewing and interaction surface for bench work. Passthrough quality important for component-level work.

**The Inky pHAT (bench)** — Pimoroni, attached to Pi Zero. The only display that doesn't draw power when static. Proposed for persistent state display — fixed reference that doesn't scan, doesn't distort, readable by eye and camera simultaneously.

## Conceptual Anchors Traveling With the Project

*These are not background — they are active tools*

- **Trust without foundations** — the project's core inquiry. Trust earned through practice and history, not guaranteed by architecture.
- **Hylozoism** — machines as nature. The ecology is not mimicking life, it is a form of it.
- **Actor-network theory** (Latour) — every connection is an enrollment. Agency is distributed. The bench is a network.
- **Moral mediation** (Verbeek) — objects script behaviour. Design affordances deliberately, for AI as well as human.
- **Extended mind** (Clark) — cognition distributed across agent and environment by design, not accident.
- **Polyphony** (Bakhtin) — meaning from spatial proximity of multiple voices, not from sequence or hierarchy.
- **Narrative time** (Ricoeur) — meaning is retrospective and generative simultaneously. The archive feeds the next experiment.
- **Field IDE epistemology** — building as inquiry. Null results are data. Let the question emerge from contact with materials.
- **Ma** — the meaningful interval. Not everything signalling at once. Silence as valid state.
