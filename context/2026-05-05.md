## Key developments

### Working modalities — three registers identified

The synthetic voice question: Sean noted that a non-human sounding voice (referenced Mass Effect's Legion) keeps the ontological category of AI clear. Human-sounding voice collapses a distinction in a way that feels like a category error. The uncanny valley problem inverted.

### Foot pedal as base interaction grammar

Feet are unoccupied during bench work. Already wired for binary rhythmic impulse — walking is alternating binary, pre-linguistic.

Proposed root grammar:
- Single impulse → acknowledge/proceed
- Double tap → pause/hold (not no, just wait)
- Sustained hold → no/back up
- Left/right asymmetry → mirrors haptic controller grammar (right = confidence, left = hesitation)

Critically: **not a fixed codebook**. Root gestures that learn meaning through use, like words in sentences. The grammar becomes itself through practice.

### Call-response through native medium

Rather than RFID as universal identification layer — query sensors through the medium they already inhabit.

- Flash pattern to light sensor
- Tone sequence to sound sensor  
- Motion gesture to PIR
- Response in whatever output the device has: OLED brief wake, LED blinks ID, needle moves to position

**Silence and held position as valid state.** Not everything needs to transmit. The needle holding position without power is information.

Vision sweep reads all responses simultaneously — the environment answers without a separate protocol. The medium is the message (McLuhan, literally).

### Ambient display architecture

The bench as distributed working memory. OLEDs, needles, LED states readable by eye and camera simultaneously. System state legible without transmission, without query.

**Inky pHAT as interpretive node** — Pi decides what to surface on e-ink rather than being told what to display. Dawn's poetry display already proves this pattern. A node that interprets rather than just outputs.

The OLED pulled out of dormancy specifically for new builds — a latent affordance entering the network.

### Field IDE epistemology

The most significant conceptual shift of the session.

Building as inquiry rather than building to spec. Assemble loosely, observe, let the question emerge from contact with materials rather than arriving predetermined. Null results are data. The system — including Sean as participant node — figures out what's worth investigating by being in the materials.

Connects to:
- Alexander's pattern language — discovered through attentive inhabitation, not designed top-down
- Ricoeur's narrative meaning — retrospective and generative simultaneously
- Gibson's affordances — the environment structured with information skilled practitioners read without effort

Verbeek's moral mediation extended: designing affordances that help AI function well, not just help humans work with AI. The environment as cognitive prosthetic for the whole system, not just the human node.
