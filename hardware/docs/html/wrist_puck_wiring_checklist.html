Wrist-Puck wiring reference

Power architecture — PowerBoost 500C

LiPo 602040→ PowerBoost BAT & GND  check polarity with multimeter first
PowerBoost 5Vo→ XIAO 5V pin — powers everything through XIAO 3V3 reg
PowerBoost 5Vo→ Servo VCC (5.2V — rated voltage for SG51R) confirmed
PowerBoost GND→ common GND rail (XIAO GND, Servo GND, DRV GND)
PowerBoost LBO→ XIAO GPIO2 (D0) — low-battery detect, INPUT_PULLUP, fires below 3.2V
USB-C charging→ PowerBoost USB input (charges LiPo in-place)
XIAO 3.3V reg powers DRV2605L — do not feed DRV from 5Vo directly.

I²C chain — XIAO → DRV2605L

XIAO 3V3→ DRV VIN
XIAO GND→ DRV GND
XIAO GPIO6 (D4)→ DRV SDA
XIAO GPIO7 (D5)→ DRV SCL
DRV2605L address: 0x5A  confirmed at 0x5A
Firmware: Wire.begin(6, 7) — must be explicit.

LRA coin motor — DRV2605L haptic pads

LRA lead A→ DRV OUT+ (haptic pad)
LRA lead B→ DRV OUT− (haptic pad)
No polarity — either orientation works.
LRA wires are short — extend before final install. Use 28–30 AWG silicone wire, pre-tin both ends. Allow enough slack to route up through the enclosure void.

Servo — SG51R

Orange (signal)→ XIAO GPIO5 (D3)
Red (VCC)→ PowerBoost 5Vo
Brown (GND)→ common GND
Servo mounts on its side, output shaft toward rail. Trim mounting tabs with flush cutters — they won't fit the pocket otherwise.
